Why Participate

Why This Public Survey Matters

Accurate grassroots data ensures that policymakers, researchers, and agritech innovators understand the ground reality faced by farmers today.

🌾

Crop Production Trends

Documenting primary crops (rice, wheat, cotton, pulses), seasonal yield patterns, and crop rotation practices.

💧

Irrigation & Water Access

Evaluating groundwater sustainability, borewells, canal water distribution, and drip/sprinkler adoption.

🧪

Fertilizer & Soil Testing

Tracking organic vs chemical nutrient usage, soil health card awareness, and fertilizer expenditure burdens.

⛅

Climate & Weather Resilience

Measuring the real impacts of droughts, erratic monsoons, unseasonal rainfalls, and extreme temperature spikes.

⚡

Power & Electricity Supply

Analyzing daily agricultural electricity availability and the operational impact of load shedding on irrigation.

💰

Financial & Credit Realities

Understanding input expenses, labour costs, bank loans, self-help groups, and credit repayment accessibility.

🚜

Modern Technology in Action

Discovering how tractors, harvester machinery, drones, and smartphone weather apps are changing fields.

⚖️

Market Fair Pricing & Losses

Examining post-harvest losses, mandi middlemen challenges, direct selling, and true farmer profitability.
